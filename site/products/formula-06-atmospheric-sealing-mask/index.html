    Description

    An occlusive overnight mask that seals the system and holds hydration against the dry air of the night. The final step.

    Key actives
    Squalane | Ceramide NP | Beta-Glucan | Cholesterol

    The Ritual
    Smooth a thin veil over the face and neck as the last step. Leave on overnight.

    Vessel
    50 ml polished titanium jar.
    Storage and Use

    Store upright, below 25°C and away from direct light. Use within six months of opening. Patch test before first use. For external use only.
